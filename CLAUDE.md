# canopy-events-ios

ARCHITECTURE.md is the reference (folder map, conventions, Decisions). The app is mocked; models follow the events and account APIs.

## Copy rules (everything people read: pages, the app, emails, docs)

- **"Canopy Account"**: always capitalized, both words, everywhere
  ("your Canopy Account", "Canopy Accounts"). The same goes for other
  Canopy product names ("Canopy Tickets", "Canopy Events").
- **US spelling**: color, not colour.
- No help text under form fields; hints go in placeholders.
